TITLE: 5 breakfast items you should never start your day with
URL: https://recipes.timesofindia.com/articles/health/5-breakfast-items-you-should-never-start-your-day-with/articleshow/103630388.cms
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

breakfast often takes a backseat as people rush to get through their mornings. However, it is essential to remember that breakfast is the most important meal of the day. It provides us with the energy and nutrients we need to kickstart our day on the right note. But not all breakfast choices are created equal. Here are five breakfast items you should never start your day with.
Sugary cereals may seem like a convenient and tasty option, especially for those with a sweet tooth. However, they are one of the worst breakfast choices you can make. These cereals are typically loaded with added sugars, artificial flavours, and colours. They may give you a quick sugar rush, but it won't be long before you experience a crash in energy levels. Instead, opt for whole-grain cereals with no added sugar and add fresh fruits for sweetness.
Pastries and doughnuts may be tempting, but they are essentially empty calories. These sugary treats are high in refined sugars and unhealthy fats, making them a poor choice for a nutritious breakfast. They offer little to no nutritional value and can lead to a spike in blood sugar levels followed by a crash, leaving you feeling sluggish and hungry soon after. Choose whole-grain options like oatmeal or whole-grain toast with nut butter for a healthier and more satisfying start to your day.
While some breakfast bars are marketed as healthy choices, many are nothing more than glorified candy bars. They often contain high amounts of sugar, artificial additives, and minimal fibre or protein. Reading the labels carefully is essential. Opt for bars that are low in sugar and rich in whole grains, nuts, and seeds to keep you full and energised.
Grabbing a breakfast sandwich from a fast-food joint on your way to work may seem convenient, but it's not the healthiest option. Most of these sandwiches are packed with sodium, unhealthy fats, and highly processed ingredients. They can contribute to weight gain and have a negative impact on your heart health. If you're in a rush, consider making a simple breakfast sandwich at home using whole-grain bread, lean protein, and fresh vegetables.
Yoghurt is often hailed as a healthy breakfast choice, but flavoured yoghurts can be deceiving. They are usually loaded with added sugars and artificial flavours. These sugars can quickly add up and lead to weight gain and other health issues. Instead, opt for plain Greek yoghurt and add your own natural sweeteners like honey or fresh fruits for a tasty and nutritious alternative.
