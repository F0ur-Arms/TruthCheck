TITLE: Foods that can naturally improve gut health
URL: https://recipes.timesofindia.com/articles/food-facts/foods-that-can-naturally-improve-gut-health/articleshow/103883362.cms
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A healthy gut is the foundation of overall well-being as it plays an important role in digestion, nutrient absorption, immunity and mental health. However, for better
gut health it is essential to incorporate the right foods into your diet. Foods rich in probiotics are naturally rich in gut friendly bacteria that helps in maintaining a balanced gut microbiome. Here are some common foods that are great for gut health and can be included in the day-to-day diet.
Yogurt Yogurt contains probiotics, which are live beneficial bacteria that can help balance the gut microbiome. Look for yogurt with "live and active cultures" on the label for the best probiotic benefits.
Kefir Kefir is a fermented milk drink that is rich in probiotics. It's similar to yogurt but has a thinner consistency and a different set of beneficial bacteria.
KimchiKimchi is a Korean fermented cabbage dish, often spiced with chili peppers and other seasonings. It's a probiotic-rich food that can be a flavorful addition to your diet.
PicklesTraditional pickles that are fermented in brine, not vinegar can provide probiotics. Check the label to ensure they are naturally fermented.
KombuchaKombucha is a fermented tea beverage that contains probiotics. It comes in various flavors and is known for its fizzy, tangy taste.
Fiber-rich foodsFiber is essential for gut health as it promotes regular bowel movements and feeds beneficial gut bacteria. Foods high in fiber include whole grains, oats, legumes, fruits, and vegetables.
Prebiotic foods Prebiotics are non-digestible fibers that serve as food for beneficial gut bacteria. Include foods like garlic, onions, leeks, asparagus, and bananas in your diet.
SauerkrautThis fermented cabbage dish is a good source of probiotics and contains vitamins and fiber that can promote gut health.
Bone brothBone broth contains collagen and gelatin, which can help repair the gut lining. It's a nourishing addition to soups and stews.
Fruits and vegetables A diet rich in colorful fruits and vegetables provides a variety of nutrients and antioxidants that can support overall gut health.
Nuts and seedsAlmonds, flaxseeds, and chia seeds are good sources of fiber and healthy fats that can benefit the gut.
Herbs and spices Some herbs and spices, like ginger and turmeric, have anti-inflammatory properties that can support a healthy gut.
