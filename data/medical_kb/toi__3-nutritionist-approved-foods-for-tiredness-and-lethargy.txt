The race against time to complete all tasks and responsibilities of life often leads to tiredness and lethargy, and sometimes it leads to major health issues. Hence, it is important to pay attention to it and look for solutions. Nutritionist Rujuta Diwekar recently shared a video on Instagram where she paid attention to the issue and also suggested three natural foods that can help handle lethargy and tiredness. Scroll down to read the details.
Aliv seed laddoo with milkAliv seed, halim, or garden cress seeds are rich in folic acid and iron and can boost hemoglobin levels. She suggests consuming them in the form of laddoo along with milk. To make laddoos, first soak these seeds in coconut water or plain water. You can add jaggery, grated coconut, and other ingredients of your choice. Eating them right before sleep helps boost the sleep cycle too.
