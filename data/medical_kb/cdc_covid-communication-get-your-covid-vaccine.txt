What to know
This page is part of a group of easy-to-read pages about COVID-19.
COVID-19 vaccines help you stay healthy
COVID-19 vaccines help keep you from getting very sick.
COVID-19 vaccines help keep you from going to the hospital.
Get your updated COVID-19 vaccines .
Ask your doctor what COVID-19 vaccines you need.
Get your updated COVID-19 vaccine
The COVID-19 virus has changed over time.
Different versions of the virus are called "variants."
An updated COVID-19 vaccine keeps you from getting very sick from new variants.
CDC recommends an updated COVID-19 vaccine for most adults ages 18 years and older.
Parents of children ages 6 months to 17 years should discuss the benefits of vaccination with a healthcare provider.
People who have allergies can get an updated COVID-19 vaccine .
People with health problems should get an updated COVID-19 vaccine .
People who have had COVID-19 should get an updated COVID-19 vaccine .
You can get an updated COVID-19 vaccine 3 months after having COVID-19.
Get an updated COVID-19 vaccine at least 2 months after your last COVID-19 shot.
