GENERAL HEALTH
The human immune system protects the body against infections.
No food or drink can instantly cure viral diseases.
Fever is a symptom of infection not a disease itself.
The body removes toxins through liver and kidneys naturally.
Detox drinks do not remove toxins from blood.
Sweating does not remove viruses from the body.
Hydration supports normal body functions.

HYDRATION
Drinking water helps digestion by aiding gastrointestinal movement.
Warm water may soothe throat irritation but does not cure infection.
Hot water does not kill viruses inside the human body.
Drinking water does not burn fat without calorie deficit.
Lemon water does not detoxify the liver.
Boiled water prevents waterborne infections if contaminated.
Overhydration can dilute sodium and be dangerous.

IMMUNITY
Vitamin C supports immune function but does not prevent infections completely.
No drink can boost immunity instantly within minutes.
Garlic has mild antimicrobial properties but cannot cure viral illness.
Turmeric has anti inflammatory properties but is not a treatment for infections.
Healthy sleep supports immune response.
Vaccination trains the immune system to recognize pathogens.
Antibiotics do not work against viruses.

RESPIRATORY ILLNESS
Steam inhalation relieves nasal congestion but does not kill viruses in lungs.
Cough syrups reduce symptoms but do not remove infection cause.
Masks do not reduce oxygen levels in healthy individuals.
Viruses spread mainly through droplets and contact.
Alcohol consumption does not prevent respiratory infection.
Cold weather alone does not cause viral infection.

Hot drinks do not prevent COVID-19 infection.
Herbal drinks cannot cure COVID-19 infection.
Vaccines reduce risk of severe disease.
5G networks do not spread viruses.
Soap destroys viruses by breaking lipid membranes.
Testing positive does not always mean severe illness.

WEIGHT LOSS
Weight loss requires calorie deficit over time.
Warm water alone does not reduce body fat.
Sweating does not equal fat loss.
Skipping meals may slow metabolism long term.
Exercise improves metabolic health.
Spot reduction of fat is not possible.

HEART HEALTH

DIABETES
Diabetes is a metabolic disorder affecting blood sugar regulation.
No herbal remedy permanently cures diabetes.

Walking helps insulin sensitivity.

MEDICINE USE
Antibiotics treat bacterial infections not viral infections.
