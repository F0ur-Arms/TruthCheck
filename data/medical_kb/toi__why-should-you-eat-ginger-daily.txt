TITLE: Why should you eat ginger daily?
URL: https://recipes.timesofindia.com/articles/food-facts/why-should-you-eat-ginger-daily/articleshow/104629659.cms
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Ginger is a quintessential part of every Indian household, from adding a punch of flavour to regular chai to making delicacies flavoursome, this aromatic and strong herbs can add soul to anything and everything! But do you know what happens when you eat ginger daily? Here are some lesser known benefits of eating ginger daily.
Anti-inflammatory properties Ginger contains bioactive compounds like gingerol that have powerful anti-inflammatory effects. Regular consumption may help reduce inflammation in the body.
Digestive health Ginger is known for its ability to alleviate nausea and aid in digestion. Consuming ginger can help reduce indigestion, bloating, and gas. It's also used to relieve motion sickness and morning sickness in pregnant women.
Boosts immunityGinger has immune-boosting properties and can help your body defend against infections. It is rich in antioxidants, which can protect your cells from oxidative stress and strengthen your immune system.
Pain reliefSome people use ginger to manage various types of pain, including menstrual pain and muscle soreness. It may have mild analgesic effects.
Cardiovascular health There is some evidence to suggest that ginger can help lower blood pressure and improve heart health. It may reduce the risk of heart disease by lowering cholesterol levels and improving blood circulation.
Weight managementGinger may aid in weight management by increasing metabolism and promoting a feeling of fullness, which can help control appetite.
Anti-nausea effectsGinger is well-known for its ability to reduce nausea, making it a useful remedy for individuals who experience nausea from motion sickness, pregnancy, or chemotherapy.
Antioxidant propertiesGinger contains antioxidants that help protect your cells from damage caused by free radicals. This can potentially reduce the risk of chronic diseases and slow down the aging process.
Anti-microbial propertiesGinger has natural anti-microbial and anti-fungal properties, which may help combat infections and support overall health.
