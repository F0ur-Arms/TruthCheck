PLANNING
Promoting healthy timing and spacing of birth and responsible parenthood is a very important task which has the potential of improving maternal and child health outcomes. As per NFHS-4, 47.8% of the married women in India are currently using modern methods of contraception. Therefore, this new section includes messages for couples for delaying birth of first child and maintaining healthy spacing between children as well, and provides information on different types of contraceptive methods available under the National Family Planning programme. It aims at: 1). Generating demand for family planning; 2). Improving knowledge about different family planning methods; both temporary and permanent methods.
