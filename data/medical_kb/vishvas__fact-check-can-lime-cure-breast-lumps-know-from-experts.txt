Fact Check: Can Limestone Cure Breast Lumps? Know From Experts
- Published: Jun 2, 2025 at 12:49 PM
New Delhi (Vishvas News): A video going viral on social media claims that a lump in a woman’s breast can be treated with limestone or chuna. The person featured in the video is Rajiv Dixit, who was associated with the Bharat Swabhiman Movement and passed away in 2010.
Vishvas News investigated the viral claim and found it to be misleading. It was found that breast lumps can be serious, so such lumps should be treated only after consulting a doctor. This is because sometimes such home remedies can have serious side effects. Sometimes such lumps can also be cancerous.
The video was shared on May 25, 2025 by an Instagram account named Dr.ninbhalli, in which Dixit can be heard saying, “If any lump has formed in any part of the body, then the best medicine is lime water. Lumps are formed due to calcium deficiency. And lime is rich in calcium. It is the cheapest source of calcium. All the lumps that form on the breasts of mothers and sisters are tumors. All of them will dissolve easily. There is lime water, mix two to three spoons of it in curd and eat. Otherwise, just eat lime. Now what is the way to eat lime? Eat it by applying it on paan. Eat it by mixing it in curd. Eat it by mixing it in water. But all this has to be done early in the morning.”

Dr Batra stated that breast lumps cannot be treated with limestone. He emphasised that if any such lumps are noticed, consult a doctor and undergo appropriate treatment.
Dr Multani, founder of Incredible Ayurveda Company and an Ayurveda expert, explains that while limestone might offer some benefits in treating small lumps, any lump, especially in the breast or other body parts, that persists over time, should be properly evaluated under medical supervision. He emphasises consulting a doctor and undergoing necessary tests to determine the severity of the lump. Relying on such videos for self-treatment can be fatal, as the lumps can be cancerous, and early diagnosis and treatment are crucial in such cases.
The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, has also issued a social media advisory, emphasising that women in our country often tend to neglect the symptoms of breast cancer. It highlighted the importance of awareness and early detection. Women are encouraged to consult healthcare professionals and get tested for non-communicable diseases at their nearest district hospital, primary health centre, or health sub-centre.
Conclusion: Vishvas News investigated the viral post and found it to be misleading. Breast lumps should never be treated with limestone. If you notice such symptoms, consult a doctor immediately for early diagnosis and treatment.
