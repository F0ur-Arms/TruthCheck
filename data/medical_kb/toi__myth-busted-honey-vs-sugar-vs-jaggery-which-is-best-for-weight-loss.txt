TITLE: Myth busted: Honey vs. Sugar vs. Jaggery, which is best for weight loss?

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

In the present time, when every other person is struggling with obesity and is looking for some remedy to lose weight, curbing your sugar intake is a generic piece of advice that everyone gives. Also, there are people who suggest shifting to jaggery or honey to keep diabetes under control and even lose weight. But the question that confuses all of us is: what is healthier, sugar, honey, or jaggery? Scroll down to find out.
Attention to the calorie gameBoth brown and white sugar have almost the same calorie density. It has been found that brown sugar is 375 calories per 100 grams, while white sugar is 390 calories per 100 grams. When it comes to honey, it has 240-330 calories per 100 grams. Lastly, if we look at jaggery, it has 380 calories per 100 grams.
10 magical benefits of using honey Honey versus jaggeryThey both have the same amount of calories, but what makes honey better than jaggery is its low glycemic index and rich nutritional level. Also, jaggery is loaded with minerals like magnesium, copper, and iron; on the other hand, honey is rich in antioxidants, vitamins B and C, and potassium.
